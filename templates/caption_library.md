# Caption & Hook Library (Copy-Paste Ready)

## High-Performing Hook Formulas (first 1–3 seconds / first line)
1. "The moment he said this changed everything…"
2. "Nobody talks about this part of [topic]…"
3. "I wasn’t ready for what he revealed next…"
4. "This is why 99% fail at [niche]…"
5. "Wait for the last 5 seconds…"
6. "They laughed until he showed the proof…"
7. "This 30-second clip will save you years…"
8. "The real reason [famous person] is winning…"

## Caption Structure (TikTok / Reels)
```
[Hook line]

[1–2 sentence context or key insight]

[Campaign CTA / hashtags exactly as required]

#requiredhashtag1 #requiredhashtag2 #fyp #viral
```

## Score Boosters
- Always put the strongest statement in the first 1.5 seconds of the video + caption.
- Use 3–5 relevant hashtags max (campaign ones first).
- Ask a question in the caption to boost comments when allowed.
