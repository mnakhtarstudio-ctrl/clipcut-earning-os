## How to Score a Campaign (Quick Filter)
Score = (Rate × Remaining Budget factor) × (1 / Competition) × Source Quality

- **Rate**: Prefer ≥ $1.00 (ideal $1.50+)
- **Budget left**: > $2,000 good; > $10k excellent
- **Min views**: ≤ 5,000 better
- **Per-clip cap**: Higher better (or none)
- **Source**: Fresh, high-energy, clear speakers → ClipCut loves it
- **Rules**: Simple hashtags, no forced faces, clear CTA
- **Platforms**: TikTok must be allowed

## Where to Find Campaigns
1. Whop Content Rewards (main marketplace)
2. Other: Vyro, Clip Collective agencies, Ssemble, DareBay, Cut.Pro championships
3. Direct brand deals (once you have results)

## Red Flags
- Any "pay to join" clipping group
- Extremely low rates (< $0.40) with high competition
- Vague source content or "use any video"
- Campaigns that demand exclusive rights or your original content
- Too many rejections without clear reason
