# SOP 02 — Weekly System Review (30–45 min, Sunday or Monday)

1. Export tracker → calculate:
   - Total clips posted
   - Approval rate
   - Average views per approved clip
   - Gross earnings vs Net (after ~10% fee)
   - Best / worst campaigns by ROI
2. Kill underperforming campaigns (low approval or low views).
3. Double down on top 2–3.
4. Review Research Agent archive for new niches.
5. Update Source Bank.
6. Decide plan upgrades or new accounts.
7. Run the Weekly Deep Review Agent for external intel.

**Weekly KPI targets**
- Approval rate > 70%
- Net RPM (revenue per 1k views after fee) improving
- At least one new high-CPM campaign tested
