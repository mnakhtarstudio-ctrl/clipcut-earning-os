# SOP 04 — Compliance & Risk Management

## Non-Negotiables
1. Only use content explicitly authorized by the campaign.
2. Follow every rule in the campaign brief (hashtags, length, CTA, no music if banned, etc.).
3. No view bots, engagement pods, or purchased views. Whop detects and bans.
4. Disclose paid partnership only if campaign requires it.
5. Keep records of every submission (screenshots + tracker).

## Fee Reality (Oct 2026)
- Content Rewards / Whop takes ~10% of most CPM payouts.
- Always calculate **net** earnings = gross × 0.90.
- Withdrawal fees may apply (ACH, crypto, etc.).

## Account Health
- Posting frequency: stay under platform spam thresholds.
- Warm new accounts gradually.
- If a campaign rejects >40% of your clips → pause and re-read rules or leave.

## Red Flags (Exit Immediately)
- Campaigns that ask you to pay to join
- Unclear source rights
- Sudden mass rejections without reason
- Platforms banning multiple accounts at once

## Backup Plan
Keep 20–30% of income in a “tool & ads” reserve (ClipCut, proxies, CapCut Pro, etc.).
