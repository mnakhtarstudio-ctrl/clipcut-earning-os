# SOP 00 — System Onboarding (First 7 Days)

Goal: From zero → first paid views in ≤ 7 days.

## Day 0 (Today – 90 min)
1. Create accounts:
   - Whop.com (free)
   - ClipCut.pro free trial (link all socials for +credits)
   - Fresh or existing TikTok + Instagram + YouTube Shorts
2. Connect social accounts inside Whop for verification.
3. Join 2–3 open free campaigns with remaining budget > $5k and CPM ≥ $1.00.
4. Download this entire `clipcut_earning_system` folder and bookmark it.
5. Open `data/earnings_tracker.csv` and replace the example row with your first test.

## Day 1–2
- Process 3–5 long videos with ClipCut.
- Post only the top 60% viral-score clips.
- Submit every post immediately.
- Log everything.

## Day 3–5
- Review approvals/rejections.
- Double volume on winning campaigns.
- Start second account if first is performing.

## Day 6–7
- First weekly review (use Weekly Agent).
- Calculate true net CPM after 10% fee.
- Decide ClipCut plan upgrade.

**Success metric**: At least 1 approved clip with >5k views and money in Whop wallet.
