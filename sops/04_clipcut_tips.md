# 04 — ClipCut Mastery (Maximize Credits & Viral Score)

## Credit Economy
- 1 credit = 1 minute of source video processed
- 60-min video ≈ 60 credits → usually 5–12 finished clips
- Free trial: 30 + up to 25 linked = 55 credits
- Creator $9/mo (annual): 250 credits
- Clipper $15/mo: 600 credits
- Pro $30/mo: ~900–1200 credits + 4K + priority

**Tip**: Batch process on annual plans (credits front-loaded yearly).

## Best Practices for High Viral Scores
1. **Source quality**: Talking-head podcasts, interviews, high-energy streams > pure gameplay or silent footage.
2. **Length**: 30–90 min sources give best variety. ClipCut handles up to ~4 hours.
3. **After generation**:
   - Sort by score descending
   - First 3 seconds must hook (question, shock, strong statement, visual)
   - Captions: Prefer animated word-synced (9 styles available)
   - Reframe: Face tracking is excellent — leave it
4. **Unique cuts**: If multi-account, re-process or slightly change caption style so posts aren't identical.
5. **Watermark**: None on any paid plan (huge advantage vs competitors).

## Workflow Speed Hacks
- Keep a "Source Bank" folder of approved YouTube links per campaign.
- Process 3–5 videos in one sitting (bulk).
- Download only top 40–50% of clips.
- Use mobile for posting while desktop processes next video.

## When to Upgrade
- Hitting credit wall → Clipper plan
- Doing agency / 3+ campaigns daily → Pro (4K looks better, priority queue)
- One viral clip often pays the whole month.

## Alternatives / Complements
- Opus Clip (more expensive, watermark on cheap plans)
- Cliptica, QuickReel, Cut.Pro, CapCut manual for specials
- Stick to ClipCut for 90% volume — it was built for Whop clippers.
